# Part-sprite proof

**Result: pass** (2026-11-10), with a change of method.

The first two attempts (parts generated one by one and scaled to fit, first for a walker, then for
side-view humanoids) were judged to look odd. The owner approved the third approach: every family is
drawn once as a whole mecha in one shared pose and cut into layers that keep their position.
All frame parts are sourced this way from now on.
