# Full Game Plan (plan 5): six phases to a complete game

**Goal:** take IRONWAGE from "campaign + arena + open contracts" to a complete handheld RPG:
deeper combat, a real garage, a three-act story, proper presentation and sound, and the meta
features a full game has.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-volledig-spel-design.md` (Dutch).

## Global constraints

Unchanged from plans 1 to 4, plus:

- **Image budget.** `scripts/art/generate.ts` writes every request to `art/ledger.json` (id, date,
  quality, method, token usage when the proxy returns it, estimated USD). It refuses to run when
  the ledger total would pass `ART_BUDGET_USD = 12`. Low quality only. Only the two image
  functions of the proxy are ever called. The proxy address and key never enter the repository.
- **Saves.** Each phase may raise the save version once. Every older version upgrades.
- **Process.** Failing test first for every rule. `timeout 250 npx vitest run`, `npx tsc --noEmit`,
  and the e2e suite must pass before a phase is pushed. One independent review (subagent, model
  `fable`, read-only) and one fix pass per phase. Draft PR per phase, stacked on the previous one.
  The APK of each phase is sent to the owner.
- **Never stuck.** The patrol stays. The last weapon cannot be sold. A build the garage accepts
  can always take a contract.
- **Text.** All player-facing text in `src/content` (strings.ts or the content file it belongs to).
  Original names only.

## Phase 1: Combat 2.0

### 1.1 New part kinds and slots

`Build` gains `booster`, `fcs`, `module`. Players must fit a booster and an FCS; a module is
optional. Enemies may leave all three out and then use the defaults below.

| Kind | Fields |
|---|---|
| `booster` | `thrust` (evasion while boosted), `cost` (fraction of turn energy to boost), weight, drain |
| `fcs` | `aim: Record<DamageType, number>`, `focus` (0..1: share of the limb penalty removed), weight, drain |
| `module` | `effect`, `power`, `uses` (per battle), weight, drain |
| `shield` | fits the left hand only; `guard` (damage multiplier while guarding), weight |

Defaults without a booster: thrust 30, cost 0.30 (today's numbers). Without an FCS: no bonus.

`FramePart.legType?: 'biped' | 'reverse' | 'quad' | 'tank' | 'hover'` (legs only, default biped):

| Type | Effect |
|---|---|
| reverse | +8 thrust |
| quad | stability ×1.25, shoulder weapons +6 aim |
| tank | stability ×1.4, cannot boost, guard multiplier 0.5 |
| hover | boost cost ×0.6, stability ×0.8 |

`Weapon` gains `impact?` (overrides the derived value), `charge?`, `melee?`, and the traits
`burst` (fires `traitPower` shots) and `salvo` (one shot at every living enemy).

Stock parts (weight 0, drain 0, so an upgraded save never becomes overweight):
`bst-stock` Stock Thrusters (30, 0.30, 100 cr), `fcs-stock` Stock FCS (no bonus, 100 cr),

### 1.2 Stability and stagger

- `staggerMax = round((60 + 0.07 × build weight) × legType factor × mods.toughness)`.
- A hit adds `impact`: the weapon's `impact`, else `damage × factor` (kinetic 1.0, explosive 1.4,
  energy 0.5). Halved against a guarding target. Each shot of a burst or twin counts.
- When the bar is full the unit is **staggered**: `staggered = 2`, charges are lost.
  While staggered: evasion 0, damage taken ×1.3, no boost, bar frozen.
- At the start of each of its own turns (skipped turns count): if staggered, `staggered -= 1`;
  at 0 the bar resets to 0. A turn begun with `staggered = 1` gets energy ×0.6.
  If not staggered, the bar drains by 35% of its maximum.
- So a stagger lasts through one whole turn of the attacker: stagger, the target's weak turn,
  one full turn to punish, recovery.

### 1.3 Guard

`guard`: costs `ceil(0.2 × turn energy)`, once a turn, not in a turn with a boost (and no boost
after guarding). Until the unit's next turn: damage ×0.6 (×0.5 on tank legs; a shield's `guard`
replaces it when lower), impact ×0.5.

### 1.4 Charge, melee, burst, salvo

- **Charge:** action `charge` pays the weapon's energy and marks the slot charged; the slot counts
  as fired that turn. Firing a charged weapon costs no energy; firing an uncharged charge weapon
  is refused (`notCharged`). A charge lasts until fired, until staggered, or (hand weapons) until
  the arms are destroyed. Heat and ammunition are paid on firing.
- **Melee:** refused (`needsBoost`) unless the unit boosted this turn. No ammunition.
- **Burst:** `traitPower` shots (twin stays as two).
- **Salvo:** one shot at every living enemy, each rolled; the aimed part on the chosen target,
  the core on the others. One ammunition, one energy cost, one heat.

### 1.5 Modules

Action `module` (painter and nothing else takes `target`). `uses` per battle, one activation a
turn, no energy cost.

| Effect | What it does |
|---|---|
| `coolant` | heat −power |
| `barrier` | absorbs the next `power` damage; gone at the start of the unit's next turn |
| `overclock` | energy +power now, heat +25 |
| `painter` | the target is painted for 3 of its own turns: +power to hit it |
| `nanites` | every working part +power% of its maximum |
| `shock` | every living enemy takes `power` impact and 15 damage to the core |
| `chaff` | −power to hit the unit until its next turn |
| `limiter` | damage ×(1 + power/100) for the rest of this turn, heat +30 |

### 1.6 Hit chance and damage, in full

```
hit    = accuracy + head aim + arms aim (hand weapons) + fcs.aim[type] + quad bonus (shoulder)
         + mods.aim + paint bonus (if target painted)
         - evasion(target) - chaff (if target has chaff)
         + PART_MODIFIER[part] × (1 - fcs.focus) - head loss penalty        clamp 10..95
evasion = 0 if legs gone or staggered, else mobility + mods.evasion + (boosted ? thrust : 0)
damage = weapon.damage × (1 - resist × (pierce ? .5 : 1)) × attacker.mods.damage × limiter
         × (target staggered ? 1.3 : 1) × (target guarding ? guard : 1)     min 1
         halved when the aimed part is already destroyed (passes to the core)
barrier absorbs before armour.
```

### 1.7 AI

Enemy, one action per call, first that applies: cool if the next shot would overheat; use a
module (barrier below 50% core, nanites below 40% average armour, others at once); boost when a
melee weapon is ready and energy covers boost + strike; fire a usable weapon (charged first);
charge an uncharged charge weapon; guard when energy is left, no boost was used and the bar is
half full or more; end turn. Skirmisher boost every third round stays.

Auto player (simulations, tests, the Auto Turn action): as today, plus the same handling of
charge, melee and guard, modules by simple thresholds, and high-impact weapons first.

### 1.8 Difficulty and Auto Turn

`settings.difficulty: 'story' | 'standard' | 'veteran'` multiplies enemy scale by 0.8, 1, 1.2.
Options screen. Default `standard`.

Battle action **Auto Turn**: the auto player plays the rest of this turn.

### 1.9 Save version 5

Adds `build.booster = 'bst-stock'`, `build.fcs = 'fcs-stock'` (and one copy of each to the
inventory) and `settings.difficulty = 'standard'`. Stored settings missing a key get its default.

### Review focus

1. A stagger, guard, barrier or limiter applied in the preview but not the resolution, or the
   reverse; counters that never clear; a state carried into the next battle of a contract.
2. Charge: paid twice, kept after the weapon is gone, fired without being charged.
3. Old saves: v1 to v4 load with a booster and an FCS and stay inside weight and energy limits.
4. The auto player or an enemy choosing an action `checkAction` refuses (a thrown error ends the
   battle screen).
5. Shields and modules counted as weapons anywhere (last weapon, `noWeapon`, the fire menu).
