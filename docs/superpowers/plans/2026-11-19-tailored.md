# Tailored Implementation Plan

**Goal:** The build is chosen at the briefing, the briefing says how the contract is likely to go in it, and one open contract asks for a class.

**Architecture:** A pure rule for what can be flown (`sortieBuilds` in `src/core/sortie.ts`), a row and a page on the briefing screen, a pure count of flights by the automatic pilot (`src/core/forecast.ts`) that the briefing runs one a frame on the set-up the battle itself uses, and a field on a rolled contract that the settlement reads.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Canvas 2D, vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-tailored-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No rule of combat changes and no balance test changes.
- A kept game loads and is as it was.
- Every new English text has its Dutch, inside the room its kind has.
- Nothing is merged without the user's word.

## Review Focus

- A saved build whose part was sold since: not offered, and never fitted.
- A saved build that is overweight, short of energy or unarmed: not offered.
- Accepting on the other side's offer, or with a wingman hired, with a saved build chosen: all three hold.
- The briefing's text rows in Dutch with the build's page open: nothing over the hint.
- A game with no saved builds: the row is there, turns to nothing, and says where builds are saved.

### Task M2: the odds

**Files:** `src/core/forecast.ts` (`FORECAST_PLAYS`, `FORECAST_STEPS`, `Forecast`, `Count`, `newCount`, `counted`, `forecastSeed`, `advance`), `src/core/ai.ts` (`stepBattle`: one action of a battle; `runBattle` is made of it), `src/core/mission.ts` (`Fight`, `startFight`; `playMission` takes the Overdrive), `src/ui/app.ts` (`fight`, `fitted`, `crewed`, `prospect`; `AppDeps.now`), `src/ui/briefingScreen.ts`, `tests/core/forecast.test.ts`, `tests/ui/odds.test.ts`, `e2e/tailored.spec.ts`

- [x] `advance(count, fight, mission, catalog, steps): Count`: the n-th flight is always the same flight; a win is a contract won to its last battle; a count comes out the same however little is worked out at a time. (A whole flight a frame, as first planned, is too much for a frame: measured.)
- [x] The battle and the odds are started from the same thing: `MissionHost` begins every battle with `startFight(app.fight(mission), ...)`, and the briefing counts on `app.prospect(offer, wingman, slot)`.
- [x] The count on the briefing: a go of eight steps a frame, more where the time can be told and there is time; kept by offer, build and wingman; the line in the header and on the build's page; nothing in the hurried mode. Dutch; a chapter of the manual; the guide; pictures.

A test was added that holds a contract's later battles to the same fight. Frame times measured in a real browser while the odds are counted, at full speed and with the processor slowed four times: one frame late in ninety at the worst.
