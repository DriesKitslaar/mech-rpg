# Tailored Implementation Plan

**Goal:** The build is chosen at the briefing, the briefing says what suits the contract, and one open contract asks for a class.

**Architecture:** A pure rule for what can be flown (`sortieBuilds` in `src/core/game.ts`), a row and a page on the briefing screen, a table of what suits each story contract held to measurement by a test, and a field on a rolled contract that the settlement reads.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Canvas 2D, vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-tailored-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No rule of combat changes and no balance test changes.
- A kept game loads and is as it was.
- Every new English text has its Dutch, inside the room its kind has.
- Nothing is merged without the user's word.

## Review Focus

- A saved build whose part was sold since: not offered, and never fitted.
- A saved build that is overweight, short of energy or unarmed: not offered.
- Accepting on the other side's offer, or with a wingman hired, with a saved build chosen: all three hold.
- The briefing's text rows in Dutch with the build's page open: nothing over the hint.
- A game with no saved builds: the row is there, turns to nothing, and says where builds are saved.
