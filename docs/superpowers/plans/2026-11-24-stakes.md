# Stakes Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An open contract can be taken on raised terms (opposition and fee half again, twice or three times, with a bond of what there is to gain), a briefing says how near its lost flights were, and the bench's further steps can be licensed for credits.

**Architecture:** A core module (`src/core/stakes.ts`; the word "terms" is taken in `economy.ts`) holds the levels, turns an offered `Mission` into the one flown (scale, freight, stake) and a bill into the bill of it. The app applies it where it applies a hired wingman (`prospect`, `startMission`), so that the count of odds and the battle are started from the same contract. The kept game gains three optional fields (`atStake`, `raised`, `bench`), read with care and written within what is read. The count of odds (`forecast.ts`) flies its lost flights again against a tenth less and keeps how many turned. The bench reads how far it goes from the telling and the licences together.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Canvas 2D at 310x270, vitest, Playwright; `scripts/careerBot.ts` and a new `scripts/stakes.ts` for the measuring.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-stakes-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Terms by level: `0` Standard, `1` Hazard, `2` Severe, `3` Ruinous; opposition and fee x1, x1.5, x2, x3; the bond is the fee times (factor - 1), rounded: none, half the fee, the fee, twice the fee.
- Terms are for the open board only: ids that begin `open-`, without an account (`grudge`), not in a lent mech.
- The fee a bond and hazard pay are figured from is the fee of the contract as flown at Standard: after a later telling's pay and a wingman's share. Rank bonus, class bonus and the pay perk stay figured from that fee.
- What a raised contract asks to be protected grows by the factor (rounded up), as `forCycle` grows it.
- A pilot who never raises the terms plays the game that was: the eighteen careers come out the same to the credit.
- A kept game from before the round loads and stays valid; every new field is optional, and what the game writes into one it reads back (`src/core/kept.ts`).
- Every text has its Dutch within the rules for text (no more than two letters longer unless listed in `ROOM_FOR`; hint lines in the one grammar); nothing lies over anything in either language.
- No new picture. Nothing is merged without the owner's word.

## Review Focus

1. **A pilot who cannot post the bond**: in debt, at nothing, one credit short, and with a wingman whose share changes the fee: the level is shown with what it asks, Accept is not taken on it, and `startMission` asked for it flies Standard and stakes nothing. (S1, S2, S4)
2. **The game closed with a raised contract in the air**: opened again, the bond is taken once and said once; opened a second time, nothing more is taken; a contract of two battles closed in the second is the same. (S2)
3. **Turning the terms while a count runs**: each level has its own count, none is mixed with another, Standard is not counted again on the way back, and Accept flies the level that is showing. (S4)
4. **Everything that pays, at once**: a later telling's pay, a wingman's share, a contract that asks for a class (with the crown's share), the rank bonus, the pay perk and hazard pay: the briefing's PAYS for the terms showing is the bill's fee plus hazard pay, and the bond is the hazard pay. (S2, S5)
5. **A kept game that says odd things of the new fields** (a bond of nothing, of a fraction, of more than a count may be; wins on raised terms for four levels; a bench licensed past five; bench work past what the licences allow): not opened; and a game the round itself wrote, opened. (S1, S6)
