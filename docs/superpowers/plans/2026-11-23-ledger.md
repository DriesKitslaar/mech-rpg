# The Ledger Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The pilots of an account get a face, an entrance, an announcement when their account opens, and a page in the database.

**Architecture:** The faces are sprites named by callsign (`GRUDGE_FACES`), carried by the words of an account and put on the lines its pilot speaks. The entrance is the one a boss has, opened by the app for the machine a notice is about, under the notice's own word. What is announced and what the page lists are pure functions of the saved game in `src/core/grudge.ts` (`accountBegun`, `ledger`); the app and the database screen only show them.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Canvas 2D at 310x270, vitest, Playwright; the art scripts in `scripts/art/` for the portraits.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-ledger-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No rule of battle and no figure of an account changes; the saved game keeps its shape (`grudge`, `accounts: string[]`).
- Pictures are made at the lowest quality, through the proxy named by `ART_PROXY_URL`; its address is never written into the repository. The round may spend half a dollar at the most; every picture is in `art/ledger.json`.
- No face is of a real person or of a character of another game.
- Every new text has its Dutch, within the room its kind has; interface strings in Dutch at most two letters longer than the English, with the same numbers in the same order.
- Nobody is listed before an account with them begins.

## Review Focus

1. A saved account whose callsign has no face (a name from another list): nothing is drawn where the face would be, and nothing breaks: the fight, the page and the announcement all go on.
2. A game that is loaded with an account already open: nothing is announced; and a meeting won, lost or walked away from announces nothing.
3. The page of an account with the longest machine name, the longest weapon name and every state (not met, met once, met twice; knows nothing, knows a weapon; due, one contract off, three off): every line of it is on the page, in English and Dutch.
4. A callsign that has come round again (closed before and open now, or closed twice): one row, and it says what is so now.
5. The entrance in a calm picture and in a hurried battle: as a boss's is there.
