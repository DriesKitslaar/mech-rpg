# Arsenal Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Five new kinds of weapon (redline, surge, punish, fork, spool), ten weapons that carry them, flown properly by the game itself and shown wherever the pilot decides.

**Architecture:** A kind is a value of `Weapon.trait`, as the six kinds are now, and the rules of each live where the rules of those do: in what a shot is worth (`previewShot`) and in what firing does (`resolveFire`). Only a spool needs something kept between turns, on the unit. The screen never works a kind out for itself: it asks the rules what a weapon would do now (`weaponNow`), and reads what a shot did off its event.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Canvas 2D at 310 x 270, vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-arsenal-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- The five kinds: `redline`, `surge`, `punish`, `fork`, `spool`. Two weapons of each, ten in all; three hand weapons, one from each client, on the shelf after that client's first contract.
- Redline: damage times `1 + heat x traitPower / 1000`, the heat being the firing machine's own as the shot is fired and never counted above `HEAT_LIMIT`.
- Surge: asks `weapon.energy` at the least, takes all there is; damage and push are the weapon's for every `SURGE_UNIT = 100` of energy taken. A kill that gives energy back gives back `weapon.energy`.
- Punish: against a staggered machine, `1 + traitPower / 100` times, on top of `STAGGER_DAMAGE`.
- Fork: a hit goes on to the next living enemy along the line after the target (round to the first again), at its core, for `traitPower` in a hundred of the damage and the push, with no roll. A miss does not leap.
- Spool: `SPOOL_BASE = 2` shots, and one more for each of the unit's own turns in a row before this one in which it was fired, up to `traitPower` more.
- No class changes: `archetypeOf` is not touched. No enemy of the story, the arena or the board carries a new weapon. No weapon that exists changes, and no constant of the rules of combat.
- A part's line is at most `PART_LINE_CHARS` (28) letters; every new text has its Dutch; an interface string in Dutch is at most two letters longer than the English and carries the same numbers in the same order.
- Pictures at quality `low` only, through the two image functions and nothing else; the proxy's address is never written into the repository.
- No balance test of what exists changes.

## Review Focus

- A surge fired with energy owed to a drain, while staggered, after an overclock, under Second Wind, and when its volley destroys something: never energy below nothing, never more given back than the least it asks, never a shot for free.
- A fork whose hit destroys its target, whose leap would land on a machine the client wants in one piece or wants driven off, on a barrier or a field, or when the first hit ended the battle: the leap follows the rules any hit follows, and there is none after the battle is over.
- A spool across a turn lost to heat, a turn in which it could not be fired, a kill that readies it again within the turn, Second Wind, and the next battle of the same contract: wound up once a turn at the most, and back to two when a turn passes without it.
- A redline fired by a machine already past the limit: worth the limit and no more. And the game's own pilot with a coolant flush: it does not vent heat a redline is about to use.
- The lines of the ten weapons in Dutch in the shop's panel with the comparison under them; the row in a battle for a surge, a spool and a redline within the list's width.

### Task R1: the rules and the ten weapons

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/core/types.ts` (the five kinds), `src/core/battle.ts`, `src/content/parts.ts` (ten weapons, their unlocks), `src/content/strings.ts`, `src/content/nl/strings.ts` (a line for each kind), `src/ui/partInfo.ts` (a surge's energy), `scripts/art/jobs.ts` (ten pictures), `src/content/sprites.json` and `public/sprites` (made by `scripts/art/process.ts`)
- Create: `tests/core/kinds.test.ts`, `tests/content/arsenal.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- `Weapon.trait` gains `'redline' | 'surge' | 'punish' | 'fork' | 'spool'`.
- `src/core/battle.ts`:
  - `SURGE_UNIT = 100`, `SPOOL_BASE = 2`.
  - `UnitState.spool: Partial<Record<WeaponSlot, number>>` (turns in a row each weapon has been fired before this one) and `UnitState.spun: WeaponSlot[]` (fired this turn, whatever a kill readied again).
  - `shotsOf(weapon)`: of a spool, `SPOOL_BASE`. `shotsNow(unit: UnitState, slot: WeaponSlot, weapon: Weapon): number`: what one pull fires as the unit stands.
  - `forkTarget(state: BattleState, attackerId: string, targetId: string): UnitState | undefined`.
  - `weaponNow(state: BattleState, unitId: string, slot: WeaponSlot, catalog: Catalog): { energy: number; shots: number; lift: number }`: what firing would take and fire as the unit stands, and `lift`, what the weapon's kind makes of its damage in a hundred (100 where it makes nothing of it; a redline's heat, a surge's energy).
  - `ShotPreview` gains `fork?: string`: the unit a hit would leap on to.
  - The `shot` event gains `via?: string` (the unit a fork's bolt leapt from; such a shot has no roll and is a hit) and `lift?: number` (what the kind made of this hit, in a hundred, when that is not a hundred: a redline fired warm, a surge, a punish landing on a staggered machine).
- `STRINGS.partInfo.traits` gains the five (a spool's is given the most it fires). A surge's energy is written with a `+` after it.
- `SHOP_PARTS` gains: `wpn-slagcaster`, `wpn-crucible` (redline), `wpn-sluice`, `wpn-spillway` (surge), `wpn-spike-driver`, `wpn-headsman` (punish), `wpn-forkbolt`, `wpn-thunderhead` (fork), `wpn-flywheel`, `wpn-thresher` (spool).

**Steps:**

- [x] Redline, punish: what a shot is worth, with tests (cold, warm, at the limit, past it; steady and staggered; with a guard and a barrier in the way).
- [x] Surge: what it takes and gives, with tests (the least it asks, all there is, what a kill gives back, Second Wind, a drain, a stagger).
- [x] Fork: the leap, with tests (who is next along the line, a miss, nothing else standing, a kill by the leap counted as any kill, the machine a contract spares, the battle ended by the first hit).
- [x] Spool: wound up and let down, with tests (each case of the review's third line).
- [x] `weaponNow` and what a preview says of a fork, with tests.
- [x] The ten weapons, their lines in both languages and their unlocks; their pictures. Tests of the catalogue: two of each kind, where each comes from, a line that fits, a picture for each.
- [ ] Mutation over the step.
