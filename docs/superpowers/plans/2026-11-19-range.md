# The Range Implementation Plan

**Goal:** A page of the simulator where the pilot's own build is flown, for nothing, against any machine met and against the pilot's own saved builds.

**Architecture:** A range fight is a contract made on the spot (`src/core/range.ts`), so that the briefing, the build row, the odds and the battle host take it as they take any other. The battle host settles it apart, as it does a drill: the bill is worked out and shown, and nothing is booked. The simulator's screen gets a second page.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Canvas 2D, vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-range-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No rule of combat changes and no balance test changes.
- A kept game loads and is as it was.
- Every new English text has its Dutch, inside the room its kind has.
- Nothing is merged without the user's word.

## Review Focus

- A range flight won, lost and walked away from: the kept game is the same but for the build fitted at the briefing.
- A machine not met: not listed, and not startable by its id.
- A mech, or a machine of the story: one at most, whatever is asked.
- The page with nothing met yet, and with every machine met: the list stays in its panel and scrolls.
- Dispatch's once-only lessons: not spent on the range.

### Task P1: the range

**Files:** `src/core/range.ts`, `src/core/economy.ts` (`Settlement.simulated`), `src/core/wingman.ts`, `src/ui/app.ts`, `src/ui/trainingScreen.ts`, `src/ui/briefingScreen.ts` (the tag), `src/ui/settlementScreen.ts`, `src/content/strings.ts`, `src/content/nl/strings.ts`, `src/content/codex.ts`, `tests/core/range.test.ts`, `tests/ui/range.test.ts`, `e2e/range.spec.ts`

**Interfaces (`src/core/range.ts`):**

- `RANGE_MAX = 3`; `isRange(mission): boolean` (id begins `range-`).
- `rangeLimit(kind: string): number`: 1 for a mech (`MECH_ENEMIES`) or a machine of the story (`STORY_ENEMIES`), `RANGE_MAX` otherwise.
- `rangeKinds(game, order: string[]): string[]`: the kinds of `order` (the database's) that are in `game.met`.
- `rangeId(kind, count): string` and `parseRange(id): { kind: string; count: number } | null`.
- `rangeMission(kind, count, text: RangeText): Mission`: one battle in the hall against `count` of `kind` (clamped to `rangeLimit`), reward 0, scale 1.
- `rangeSettlement(bill: Settlement): Settlement`: the bill as it would have been, marked `simulated`.

- [ ] The rules above, with tests: the limit, the kinds in order, ids there and back, the mission, the settlement.
- [ ] `App`: `rangeList()`, `openRangeBriefing(kind, count)`, range ids in `offered`, back to the range from briefing and settlement. A machine not met starts nothing.
- [ ] The battle host: a range flight has the simulator's voice (no lessons, no name), bills and books nothing, and shows the bill it would have had. Tests: the kept game before and after a flight won, lost and walked away from.
- [ ] The simulator's second page: L1/R1, the list, how many, the machine and what the database says. The briefing's tag; no wingman. The settlement marked as not billed.
- [ ] Dutch; a chapter of the manual; the guide; browser test with pictures; mutation; APK.
