# Launch Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every contract flown from the hangar opens with a short launch of the pilot's own machine, which one press cuts short.

**Architecture:** The launch is the first stage of the contract's own screen (`MissionHost` in `src/ui/app.ts`): while a `LaunchScene` is alive it is updated, drawn and pressed in the battle's place, and the battle does not move. The rule for which contracts launch is a pure function in `src/core/launch.ts`. The scene draws one new background picture, the pilot's machine from behind, and light laid over both.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Canvas 2D at 310 x 270, vitest, Playwright; the art pipeline in `scripts/art`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-launch-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- About 1.5 s in all: `LAUNCH_READY_MS = 700`, `LAUNCH_GO_MS = 800`, the light over the battle after it `LAUNCH_GLOW_MS = 200`.
- Not shown where `deps.fast` is set, nor where `settings.fastBattles` is on.
- Shown for the first battle only, and only where `launches(mission)` holds.
- A or B ends it at once; the press does nothing else.
- In a calm picture: no shake, no white, lines of light that stand still.
- One picture, `launch-tunnel`, low quality; the ledger's ceiling stays 14 dollars.
- Every new text has its Dutch; interface strings at most two letters longer than the English.
- No rule of combat, no number and no kept game changes.

## Review Focus

- A press during the launch that reaches the battle under it (a turn taken unseen, a banner dismissed, a row picked).
- The battle's clock, banner, music or first message moving while the launch is still on the screen.
- A contract of several battles: a launch before the second battle.
- A build whose back-view sprites are missing a layer (an empty hand, no shoulder weapon): the scene must draw what there is.
- The scene in a hurried game: it must not exist at all, so that no test of a contract waits for it.

### Task L1: the launch

**Files:**
- Create: `src/core/launch.ts`, `src/ui/launch.ts`, `tests/core/launch.test.ts`, `tests/ui/launch.test.ts`, `e2e/launch.spec.ts`
- Modify: `scripts/art/jobs.ts` (the picture's job), `src/content/sprites.json` and `public/sprites/bg-launch.png` (made by the pipeline), `src/platform/audio.ts` (two sounds), `src/content/strings.ts`, `src/content/nl/strings.ts`, `src/ui/app.ts` (`MissionHost`), `src/content/codex.ts` and `src/content/nl/reference.ts` (a sentence of the manual), `docs/handleiding.md`, `README.md`

**Interfaces:**
- `src/core/launch.ts`: `launches(mission: Pick<Mission, 'id' | 'arenaRank' | 'loaner'>): boolean`; `launchRumble(weight: number): number` (60 to 160 ms, rising with weight from 700 to 1700).
- `src/ui/launch.ts`: `LAUNCH_READY_MS`, `LAUNCH_GO_MS`, `LAUNCH_MS`, `LAUNCH_GLOW_MS`, `LAMPS_AT: [number, number, number]`, `class LaunchScene { constructor(sprites, build, paint, opts: LaunchOpts); update(ms); done: boolean; draw(ctx); view(): { ms: number; stage: 'ready' | 'go'; lamps: number } }`, `drawLaunchVeil(ctx, strength: number, calm: boolean)`. (As built: a press ends the launch in the contract's screen itself, which simply drops the scene, so the scene has no `skip` or `cut`.)
- `MissionHost.view()` gains `launch: { ms, stage, lamps } | null`.
- Sounds `'lamp'` and `'catapult'`; strings `STRINGS.catapult = { bay, go }` (`launch` was taken: a carrier's message). The picture is `launch-tunnel`, not `bg-launch`: it is no background a battle is fought in front of.
