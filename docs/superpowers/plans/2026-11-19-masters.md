# The Masters Implementation Plan

**Goal:** A second page of the arena with seven masters, one of each class; beating one in a machine of their own class takes that class's crown, which is seen, worn as a paint, and paid for on the board.

**Architecture:** A master's duel is a contract made on the spot (`src/core/masters.ts`), as an arena fight is, so the briefing, the build row, the odds and the battle host take it as they take any other. Its id begins `arena-`, so what already holds for the arena by id (no wingman) holds for it. The masters themselves are content (`src/content/masters.ts`): a machine of parts, a paint, two figures of strength and their words. The crown is booked where a cup is.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Canvas 2D, vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-masters-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No rule of combat changes and no balance test of what exists changes.
- A kept game loads and is as it was; `masters` and `crowns` are optional in it and stay when the story starts over.
- Every new English text has its Dutch, inside the room its kind has; an interface string in Dutch is at most two letters longer than the English.
- No pictures are bought.
- Nothing is merged without the user's word.

## Review Focus

- A master beaten in a machine of another class: the purse, and no crown. Beaten again as the class: the crown, and a quarter of the purse. A crown is never given twice.
- The class that counts is that of the build the duel was flown in: a saved build turned to at the briefing, not the one that stood on the floor when the briefing opened.
- A pilot who is not champion: the page is locked, and no id starts a master's duel.
- A kept game from before the round, and one with rubbish where the crowns are kept: the first loads with nothing held, the second is refused or read as nothing held.
- The page and the briefing with the longest names there are, in Dutch: nothing overlaps and nothing runs out of its panel.

### Task K1: the masters

**Files:** `src/core/masters.ts` (new), `src/core/archetype.ts` (`CLASS_AI`, moved from `range.ts`), `src/core/range.ts`, `src/core/campaign.ts` (`Mission.crown`), `src/core/game.ts` (`masters`, `crowns`), `src/core/economy.ts` (`Settlement.crowned`), `src/content/masters.ts` (new), `src/content/arena.ts` (`ALL_ENEMIES`), `src/content/nl/*`, `src/content/strings.ts`, `src/content/codex.ts` (a chapter of the manual), `src/ui/paint.ts` (seven schemes), `src/ui/app.ts`, `src/ui/arenaScreen.ts`, `src/ui/briefingScreen.ts`, `src/ui/settlementScreen.ts`, `src/ui/soundtrack.ts`, `tests/core/masters.test.ts`, `tests/content/masters.test.ts`, `tests/ui/masters.test.ts`, `e2e/masters.spec.ts`, `docs/handleiding.md`, `README.md`

**Interfaces (`src/core/masters.ts`):**

- `MASTER_PURSE = 9000`.
- `interface Master { kind: Archetype; name: string; build: Build; paint: string; scale: number; plating?: number; style: string; taunt: string; concede: string }`.
- `masterId(kind): string` = `arena-master-<kind>`; `parseMaster(id): Archetype | null`; `isMaster(mission): boolean`.
- `mastersOpen(state): boolean`: `state.arenaRank >= CHAMPION_RANK`.
- `masterBeaten(state, kind): boolean`, `crownHeld(state, kind): boolean`, `crownsHeld(state): number`: unknown names in the kept lists count for nothing.
- `masterPurse(state, kind): number`: the purse, a quarter of it once that master is beaten.
- `masterEnemy(master): EnemyDef`: id `masterId(kind)`, their build, `ai: CLASS_AI[kind]`, composed, their paint, their plating.
- `masterMission(state, master, text: { client: string; win: string }): Mission`: one battle on `bg-arena`, `scale: master.scale`, `reward: masterPurse`, `crown: master.kind`, briefing `[style, "taunt"]`.
- `recordMasterWin(state, kind, flownAs: Archetype | null): GameState`: the master is beaten; the crown is held when `flownAs === kind`. Idempotent.
- `crownSettlement(settlement, mission, flownAs, state): Settlement`: marks `crowned: kind` on a won settlement of a master's duel flown as the class, when the crown was not held yet.

**Steps:**

- [ ] The rules above, with tests: ids there and back, open to the champion only, purse and its quarter, the record (beaten without a crown, the crown, never twice, unknown names), the contract (one battle, the arena, the scale, the crown it names), the settlement's mark.
- [ ] `CLASS_AI` in `archetype.ts`; the range uses it under that name.
- [ ] Kept game: `masters?: string[]`, `crowns?: string[]`, validated as the cups are; kept across a new cycle. Tests: a game from before loads; rubbish is refused.
- [ ] The seven masters as content: each machine is of its class by `archetypeOf`, may deploy by the pilot's own rules, and is made of parts that have pictures; seven names unlike any on the ladder or in the story; seven paints unlike each other and unlike the ladder's (`PaintScheme.crown` names the class whose master wears it; the garage does not offer such a scheme to anyone yet: that is K2's). `ALL_ENEMIES` knows them.
- [ ] Strength, by measurement (the script is kept in `scripts/masters.ts`): the two figures of each master, and a test per master that holds the measure as ranges: the late build of the class with a veteran's perks, with none, and with all; and that the duel is over in a reasonable number of rounds.
- [ ] `App`: `masterFight`, `openMasterBriefing(kind)`, the ids in `offered`, back to the masters' page from briefing and settlement, the arena's music; booking: purse, `recordMasterWin` with the class flown, the crown's notice. Tests: by every road, champion or not; the saved build chosen at the briefing decides.
- [ ] The arena's second page: L1/R1, the seven rows, the master beside the list, the detail and its four states, locked until champion; the rank moves into the list's panel to make room for the tabs. The briefing's tag and the crown's line. The settlement's row and the master's word. Tests with the drawn page; nothing overlaps.
- [ ] Dutch; a chapter of the manual; the guide; README; browser test with pictures.
- [ ] Mutation over the step.
- [ ] APK.

### Task K2: what a crown gives

**Interfaces:**

- `CROWN_BONUS = 0.5`; `askBonus(state, kind): number`: `CROWN_BONUS` for the holder of that class's crown, `ASK_BONUS` otherwise. `askSettlement` takes the rate.

**Steps:**

- [ ] The board pays the holder of a crown half as much again for a contract that asks for that class; the briefing's line and the settlement's row say the figure. Tests; the board's own tests do not change.
- [ ] The two medals, with their Dutch; the counts of medals in tests follow.
- [ ] Dutch; the manual; the guide; browser test with pictures.
- [ ] Mutation over the step.
