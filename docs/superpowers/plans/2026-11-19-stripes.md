# Stripes Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A class gives the pilot a stripe at 3, 8 and 15 contracts won flying it, and each stripe adds to one of the pilot's skills in every machine.

**Architecture:** Stripes are read off the record the game already keeps (`wonAs`); nothing new is stored. The rules are pure functions in `src/core/stripes.ts`; which skill each class trains is content (`src/content/stripes.ts`). What stripes add is summed into the pilot's perk effects in the two places the app asks for those (`App.perks()` for bills, `App.fight()` for battles), so the odds on a briefing count them without knowing of them.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Canvas 2D at 310 x 270, vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-stripes-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- A stripe at 3, at 8 and at 15 contracts won as a class: `STRIPES_AT = [3, 8, 15]`.
- What a stripe adds: duellist energy +10; marksman aim +2; artillery costs -4%; lancer cooling +3; bastion armour +3%; skirmisher evasion +2; all-rounder pay +3%.
- Added to what perks give, in every battle the perks count in and on every bill. Not in a drill, on the range's lent frames, or in the Gauntlet: wherever perks are left outside, stripes are.
- Nothing is stored. A kept game from before the round has the stripes its record has earned.
- Every new text has its Dutch; interface strings at most two letters longer than the English.
- No balance test of what exists changes: the measuring scripts fly pilots without stripes unless asked.

## Review Focus

- A record that is odd (a count that is no number, negative, or of a class nobody knows): no stripe, no crash.
- Stripes counted twice (once in `perks()` and again by a caller that adds them itself), or left out of the briefing's odds while the battle has them.
- The pilot told of stripes already held every time the game is opened, or never told of the ones a game from before the round arrives with.
- A stripe for a win in a lent machine or in the simulator.
- Rows of the classes page and the perks page in Dutch: the longest class and perk names beside the new marks and words.

### Task T1: the rules, and stripes in the fight and on the bill

**Files:**
- Create: `src/core/stripes.ts`, `src/content/stripes.ts`, `tests/core/stripes.test.ts`, `tests/content/stripes.test.ts`
- Modify: `src/ui/app.ts` (`perks()`, `fight()`), `scripts/masters.ts` (an option to measure a pilot with every stripe), `tests/ui/` (through the app)

**Interfaces:**
- `src/core/stripes.ts`: `STRIPES_AT`, `MAX_STRIPES = 3`, `stripesOf(game: Pick<GameState, 'wonAs'>, kind: Archetype): number`, `nextStripeAt(game, kind): number | null`, `stripesHeld(game): number`, `interface Trained { kind: Archetype; perk: string; perStripe: Partial<PerkEffects> }`, `stripeEffects(game, trained: Trained[]): PerkEffects`, `addEffects(a: PerkEffects, b: PerkEffects): PerkEffects`, `stripesEarned(before, after): { kind: Archetype; stripe: number }[]`.
- `src/content/stripes.ts`: `TRAINED: Trained[]` (one a class, in the classes' order; `perk` is the id of the perk that raises the same skill).
- `App.perks()` returns perks plus stripes; `App.fight()` takes its mods from the same sum.

**Steps:**

- [ ] The rules with their tests: the thresholds, an odd record, the sum, what was earned between two states.
- [ ] The content with its tests: every class trains one skill, each a different one, each the skill of a perk there is; the figures above.
- [ ] In the app: a battle and a bill have them; a drill, the range's lent frames and the Gauntlet have not; the briefing's odds are the battle's. Tests.
- [ ] Measured: the seat and the masters for a pilot with every perk and every stripe, beside one without stripes. Written down in the plan; halved if a fight has become a formality.
- [ ] Mutation over the step.
