# Stripes Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A class gives the pilot a stripe at 3, 8 and 15 contracts won flying it, and every fifth stripe held is a perk point.

**Architecture:** Stripes are read off the record the game already keeps (`wonAs`); nothing new is stored. The rules are pure functions in `src/core/stripes.ts`. The points stripes bring are counted where perk points are counted (`perkPoints` in `src/core/pilot.ts`), so every screen that shows or spends points has them without knowing of stripes.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Canvas 2D at 310 x 270, vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-stripes-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- A stripe at 3, at 8 and at 15 contracts won as a class: `STRIPES_AT = [3, 8, 15]`.
- A perk point for every five stripes held, of whatever classes: `STRIPES_A_POINT = 5`, four points at most.
- A stripe gives nothing in a fight by itself: no battle option and no bill changes for it.
- A win counts for a class where `countsForClass(mission)` holds: anything flown in a build of the pilot's own but the simulator's fights and a floor of the Gauntlet.
- Nothing is stored. A kept game from before the round has the stripes its record has earned.
- Every new text has its Dutch; interface strings at most two letters longer than the English.
- No balance test of what exists changes.

## Review Focus

- A record that is odd (a count that is no number, negative, or of a class nobody knows): no stripe, no point, no crash.
- Points counted twice, or a kept game whose perks were bought with stripe points read as holding more than it may.
- The pilot told of stripes already held every time the game is opened. (A game from before the round arrives with its stripes unannounced: the hangar marks the points to spend.)
- A stripe for a win in a lent machine or in the simulator.
- Rows of the classes page and the perks page in Dutch: the longest class names beside the new marks and words.
